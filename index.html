Invitation to INPHYNITT'25
The Grandest Physics Fest in Trichy!

We are thrilled to invite you to INPHYNITT’25, the biggest physics department fest in Trichy. This flagship event is a celebration of science, innovation, and curiosity, featuring a wide range of expert speaker sessions and competitios along with entertaining events that promise to inspire and challenge young minds.

Join us as we bring together students from across the country to explore the fascinating world of physics, connect with like-minded peers, and experience an event like no other. Whether you’re here to compete, learn, or simply immerse yourself in the excitement, INPHYNITT’25 has something for everyone!

Mark your calendars for March 7th, and be a part of this extraordinary journey of knowledge and discovery.

Looking forward to seeing you at INPHYNITT’25!
